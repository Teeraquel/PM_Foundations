# GTM Launch Plan, StreamLine (B2C)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Feature | Spotlight Curated Rail |
| Goal | Engagement |
| Launch tier | M, Targeted |

## Goal & Audience
- **Goal:** Engagement, Retention increase user retention in a market where smaller, more specialized competitors threaten StreamLine's market position
- **Target audience:** Current subscribers with at least 2 homepage sessions in the last 30 days that ended without a play start

## Launch Tier
- **M, Targeted**, Retain subscribers by enhancing the homepage to include recommended picks for current subscribers with at least 2 homepage sessions in the last 30 days that ended without a play start, reducing their need to search titles

Stage 1 (tier S): Spotlight live for 50% of the target segment, 50% held out, for two full billing cycles, with no public activity. Stage 2 (tier M): full rollout and weekly picks posts, only if Stage 1 meets the retention target with guardrails intact.

## Channels
1. **Owned: In-app: one-line label on the Spotlight rail, shown to test-group users on their first 3 homepage views**
2. **Owned: Email: re-engagement email to test-group users who haven't opened the app in 14+ days, featuring 3 Spotlight picks with a deep link to each title. Owner: Marketing**
3. **Owned: Stage 2: weekly Spotlight picks post featuring 3 titles, linking into the app**

## Enablement & Assets
label copy, re-engagement email, and weekly post copy plus artwork. First 4 weekly curated lists. Brief Support on the product before launch

## Ownership, Budget & Timeline
- **Ownership & budget:** PM owns the plan, Support FAQ and briefing + post-launch metrics. Marketing owns email, weekly spotlight picks posts. Eng owns start of Stage 1, in-app enhancement,  app store submission, A/B holdout and tracking
Content Editor owns picking titles/weekly curation to include 6+ kid safe titles; checking licensing. Support briefed, no extra budget. weekly metrics readout
Executive Sponsor - stage 2 go/no-go call
- **Timeline:** Week 1: Holdout split and event tracking built
Week 2: app-store submission. First 4 curated lists ready, licensing checked ; Support FAQ and briefing
Week 3: start Stage 1. 
Week 4- 11: monitor, collect feedback; Weekly curation refresh; weekly metrics readout
Week 11: Go/no-go for Stage 2

## Success Metrics
- **Metrics:** Month-over-month retention in the test group is at least 1 percentage point higher than the holdout, against a current baseline of 90% retention (10% monthly churn).  

total weekly viewing hours per subscriber test vs. holdout - no more than 2% below holdout
Homepage sessions ending without a play, test vs. holdout -15% by day 14
- **Bad signal to watch for:** Early warning is Spotlight play starts divided by Spotlight impressions, read weekly. If it stays below the top algorithmic rail for 2 straight weeks, the editor refreshes the curation approach. Viewing hours: if test falls more than 2% below holdout for 2 straight weeks, the PM pauses Stage 1. Sessions without a play: if the drop is under 5% at day 14 (Week 5), the PM and editor review picks and rail position.
- **Likely post-launch decision:** iterate - adjust messaging
