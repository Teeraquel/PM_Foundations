Slide 05 · Strategy

Problem hook & value proposition

Problem hook

If StreamLine doesn't launch StreamLine Spotlight, customer engagement will continue to plateau, the company will lose subscribers—and its market-leading position—to smaller, more specialized competitors who can better meet our customer's specific viewing demands.

Value proposition

StreamLine Spotlight, offers our subscribers a personalized curated cinematic experience because our users are our greatest assets —without them we lose our market-leading position

Strategic crisis

Engagement will continue to plateau, and the company will lose subscribers—and its market-leading position—to smaller, specialized competitors

Moment of misery

Users will choose smaller more specialized streaming providers who can meet their specific viewing demands

Data-backed hypothesis

Based on subscriber retention data and cohort analysis, I believe that improving the search experience for the Casual Browser wanting to trust in-app recommendations will result in an increase in subscriber retention, as measured by a 4% change in Subscriber Retention I will protect subscriber retention and will make a go/no-go decision after 3 months.

1 · Open

Entry

user opens the app to watch something and a small set of suggested picks is displayed + neutral to optimistic about their experience but open to the suggestions + replaces the open ended browsing view

2 · Evaluate

Evaluate Top Picks

user recognizes specificity of the top 3 suggested picks + decides to actively test the relevancy by choosing to watch a recommended pick + resolves the "I'm not a genre, I'm a person" complaint

3 · Commit

Commit & Watch

user is able to select a title quickly and begin watching with no blockers + this reduces cognitive fatigue by reducing the decision-making burden + removes the frustrations raised about feeling overwhelmed by having to scroll through too many titles

4 · Return

Reinforce Loyalty

user returns expecting the same quality experience + users begin to trust the app again + users lose interest in competitor's options

Vision

Every StreamLine subscriber finds something they'll love within seconds of opening the app, without ever needing to search.

Success: month-over-month subscriber retention rate Guardrail: total weekly viewing hours per subscriber
View prototype
Functional requirements
  • The Spotlight rail is fully visible without scrolling at 1280×720 (desktop) and 390×844 (mobile).
  • The rail shows at least 6 and at most 10 titles.
  • On a kids' profile, 0 titles rated above the profile's maturity limit appear in the rail.
  • When fewer than 6 curated titles pass the availability, maturity, and watched checks, the rail is replaced by the fallback algorithmic rail in the same slot, with no empty state shown.
  • A user can go from homepage to playback in 2 taps or fewer (card, then Play).
MoSCoW scope
  • Must: Rail placement above the fold on the homepage; Lightweight editorial tool to pick and order titles
  • Should: Hide already-watched titles; One-line editorial hook per title
  • Could: Trailer preview on focus
  • Won't (now): Per-user personalization of the rail
Evals
  • Accuracy: 100% of rendered titles pass all checks
  • Time-on-task: median of 20 seconds or less, and at least 30% faster than control
  • Safety: 0 violations

We will protect weekly viewing hours per subscriber throughout the test.

Slide 09 · Launch

Go-to-market & success metrics

Goal: Engagement, Retention Tier: M, Targeted Stage 1: tier S Stage 2: tier M
Audience

Current subscribers with at least 2 homepage sessions in the last 30 days that ended without a play start

Staging

Stage 1 (tier S): Spotlight live for 50% of the target segment, 50% held out, for two full billing cycles, with no public activity. Stage 2 (tier M): full rollout and weekly picks posts, only if Stage 1 meets the retention target with guardrails intact.

Channels
Owned · In-app: one-line label on the Spotlight rail, shown to test-group users on their first 3 homepage views Owned · Email: re-engagement email to test-group users who haven't opened the app in 14+ days, featuring 3 Spotlight picks with a deep link to each title Owned · Stage 2: weekly Spotlight picks post featuring 3 titles, linking into the app
Primary · Retention+1 pt

Month-over-month retention in the test group is at least 1 percentage point higher than the holdout, against a current baseline of 90% retention (10% monthly churn).

Guardrail · Viewing hours≤ −2%

total weekly viewing hours per subscriber test vs. holdout - no more than 2% below holdout

Leading · Sessions without a play−15%

Homepage sessions ending without a play, test vs. holdout -15% by day 14

Bad signal to watch for
  • Early warning is Spotlight play starts divided by Spotlight impressions, read weekly. If it stays below the top algorithmic rail for 2 straight weeks, the editor refreshes the curation approach.
  • Viewing hours: if test falls more than 2% below holdout for 2 straight weeks, the PM pauses Stage 1.
  • Sessions without a play: if the drop is under 5% at day 14 (Week 5), the PM and editor review picks and rail position.

Likely post-launch decision: iterate - adjust messaging
