# Problem Hook & Value Proposition, Module 1

- **Scenario:** StreamLine Spotlight (B2C)

## Strategic crisis
Engagement will continue to plateau, and the company will lose subscribers—and its market-leading position—to smaller, specialized competitors

## Moment of misery
Users  will choose smaller more specialized streaming providers who can meet their specific viewing demands

## Problem hook
If StreamLine doesn't  launch StreamLine Spotlight,  engagement will continue to plateau,  the company will lose subscribers—and its market-leading position—to smaller, more specialized competitors who can meet their specific viewing demands

## Value proposition
For StreamLine users, we will create StreamLine Spotlight to a curated cinematic experience  because our users are our greatest assets —without them we lose our market-leading position.
