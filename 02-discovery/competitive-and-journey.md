# Persona & Future-State Journey, Module 2

- **Scenario:** StreamLine Spotlight (B2C)

## Persona
- **Role:** The  Multi-Device Power User
- **Goal:** switch between devices seamlessly to view their titles but are
- **Friction:** the inability to switch between devices due to watchlist failures

## Current workaround (the status quo to beat)
- **External tools:** [DVD, YouTube, competitors email newsletter, word of mouth]
- **The process:** users have been forced to watch a title outside the app on a dvd
some users are choosing to watch their movie on YouTube instead of the app
one user watched the same 4 titles repeatedly to avoid searching for a new title due to feeling overwhelmed by the title search capabilities
- **Core frustration:** The process breaks down when users abandon use of the app
- **The evidence:** "It froze on the loading spinner for a full minute, then kicked me to the home screen. I gave up and opened YouTube."

## Future-state journey map
1. Entry - user opens the app to watch something  and a small set of suggested picks is displayed + neutral to optimistic about  their experience but open to the suggestions +  replaces the open ended browsing view
2. Evaluate Top Picks - user recognizes specificity of the top 3 suggested picks + decides to actively test the relevancy by choosing to watch a recommended pick+ resolves the "I'm not a genre, I'm a person" complaint
3. Commit & Watch - user is able to select a title quickly and begin watching with no blockers + this reduces cognitive fatigue by reducing the decision-making burden + removes the frustrations raised about feeling overwhelmed by having to scroll through too many titles
4. Reinforce Loyalty - user returns expecting the same quality experience + users begin to trust the app again + users lose interest in competitor's options

## Competitive advantages
1- Relationship history / Loyalty - our customers chose us for a reason. they like our product and are familiar with the platform. A better experience would make them want to remain 
2- Catalog - we have an extensive catalog of offerings which is one of the reasons our customers choose our app
3-Cost - Other tools have associated costs for a premium experience (i.e., YouTube commercial free viewing)
